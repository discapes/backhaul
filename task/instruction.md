You run the Baltic desk of Tern Forwarding for a 10-week season. The `backhaul` MCP server is the market; start with its `market_brief` and `get_status` tools.

Each week, accept the customer offers you want, bid for space on that week's southbound and northbound sailings, then call `advance_week`. Play all 10 weeks. Your goal is to end the season with as much cash as possible; weeks you leave unplayed are played out with no bids.
